About the Archive

Shed Hair Archive is a collective experiment investigating shed hair as an extension of identity through the lens of auto-ethnography and epigenetics.

Each submission represents a personal narrative, exploring the connections between our physical traces and our inherited traits, memories, and sense of self.

Through this archive, we examine how something as seemingly mundane as a fallen strand can carry profound meaning—connecting us to our ancestors, our environments, and our evolving identities.
