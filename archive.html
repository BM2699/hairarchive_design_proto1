ABOUT

Matrilineal Hair Archive is a collective experiment investigating shed hair as an extension of identity through the lens of auto-ethnography and epigenetic.

The following archive is the result of a 2-day crowd-sourced installation that took place in Brooklyn, N.Y., Spring 2025.

Participants were asked to:

  1. contribute a sample of their hair
  2. write down a physical, spiritual, emotional, or habitual trait they believe inherited from their maternal side

Each submission represents a personal narrative, exploring the connections between our physical traces and our maternal inheritance, memories, and sense of self.

This archive is curious about:

  1. What is shed hair beyond waste, resource, and evidence?
  2. Who decides what is worthy for preservation?
  3. How do we, and can we, negotiate the terms for our data usage?
